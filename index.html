Kol Koi Pondscapes is a koi pond and water garden store and pond service in historic downtown Kennesaw. Since 2006 we have made ponding and birding fun and affordable.

Owner Kristie Lane moved into a new house in 2006 and inherited a pond. She found she loved ponds and koi, and opened Kol Koi so other people could learn to keep a pond and enjoy it.

Whether you are starting from scratch or got a pond with the house, the staff onsite will answer your questions.
